Front wall is at the top; 0,0 is the front-left corner. Click or drag to aim.

Calibrate a head: use the board to aim it at a known floor point, click that point on the plan, press Record. Do 3–4 spread-out points, then Solve. Uncalibrated heads use a guessed orientation (hung, pan 0 facing into the hall) and will point the wrong way.
